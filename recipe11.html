Omelette

Prep Time: 5 minutes Cook Time: 8 minutes Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons milk or water
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, Swiss, or your choice)
  • 1/4 cup diced vegetables (bell peppers, mushrooms, onions)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh herbs (chives, parsley, or basil)
  • 1/4 cup diced ham or cooked bacon (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, salt, and pepper until well combined.
  2. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add butter.
  3. Once butter is melted and bubbling, pour in the egg mixture.
  4. Let the eggs cook undisturbed for 30 seconds until edges start to set.
  5. Using a spatula, gently push the cooked edges toward the center, tilting the pan to let uncooked eggs flow to the edges.
  6. When eggs are mostly set but still slightly runny on top, add cheese, vegetables, and herbs to one half.
  7. Fold the empty half over the filled half using the spatula.
  8. Cook for another 30 seconds to 1 minute until cheese melts.
  9. Slide the omelette onto a plate and serve immediately.
