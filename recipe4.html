Grilled Salmon

Prep Time: 15 minutes Cook Time: 12 minutes Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • 4 sprigs fresh dill
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon paprika

Instructions

  1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (400-450°F).
  2. Pat salmon fillets dry with paper towels.
  3. In a small bowl, mix olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, salt, pepper, and paprika.
  4. Brush the mixture over both sides of the salmon fillets.
  5. Place lemon slices and dill sprigs on the grill grates.
  6. Place salmon fillets skin-side down on the lemon slices.
  7. Grill for 6-8 minutes, then carefully flip and grill for 4-6 minutes more.
  8. Remove from grill when internal temperature reaches 145°F.
  9. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving with additional lemon wedges.
