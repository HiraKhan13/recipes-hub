Veggie Stir Fry

Prep Time: 15 minutes Cook Time: 10 minutes Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 2 green onions, chopped

Instructions

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
  2. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add bell pepper and carrot, stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Add broccoli and snap peas, continue stir-frying for 3 minutes.
  5. Add soy sauce, sesame oil, and red pepper flakes.
  6. Stir-fry for another 2 minutes until vegetables are crisp-tender.
  7. Garnish with green onions and serve hot over rice.
