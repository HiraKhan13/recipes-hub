Caprese Salad

Prep Time: 10 minutes Cook Time: 0 minutes Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 large ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, minced (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon honey (optional, for sweet balsamic)

Instructions

  1. Arrange tomato slices on a large serving platter in a single layer.
  2. Place a slice of mozzarella on top of each tomato slice.
  3. Tuck fresh basil leaves between the tomato and mozzarella layers.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt, pepper, and garlic if using.
  5. If you prefer a sweeter dressing, add honey to the mixture and whisk until dissolved.
  6. Drizzle the dressing over the arranged salad.
  7. Let the salad sit at room temperature for 5-10 minutes to allow flavors to meld.
  8. Serve immediately as a refreshing appetizer or side dish.
