Chicken Alfredo

Prep Time: 15 minutes Cook Time: 20 minutes Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound fettuccine pasta
  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup cream cheese
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1/4 cup reserved pasta water

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to boil and cook fettuccine according to package directions.
  2. While pasta cooks, season chicken with salt and pepper.
  3. In a large skillet, melt 1 tablespoon butter over medium-high heat.
  4. Add chicken and cook for 6-8 minutes until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  5. In the same skillet, melt remaining butter and add garlic. Cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  6. Add heavy cream and bring to a simmer. Cook for 2-3 minutes until slightly thickened.
  7. Add Parmesan cheese and cream cheese, stirring until melted and smooth.
  8. Add oregano, salt, and pepper. Stir in reserved pasta water if needed for consistency.
  9. Return chicken to skillet and add cooked pasta. Toss until well coated.
  10. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.
