Fruit Tart

Prep Time: 30 minutes Cook Time: 25 minutes Servings: 8

Ingredients

  • For the Crust:
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cold butter, cubed
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 tablespoons ice water
  • For the Custard:
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • For the Topping:
  • 2 cups mixed fresh fruits (strawberries, kiwi, blueberries, etc.)
  • 1/4 cup apricot jam, warmed

Instructions

  1. Make the Crust: In a food processor, pulse flour, butter, and sugar until crumbly.
  2. Add egg yolk and ice water, pulse until dough forms. Wrap in plastic and chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Roll out dough and press into a 9-inch tart pan. Prick with fork and bake at 375°F for 15 minutes.
  4. Make the Custard: In a saucepan, heat milk until steaming.
  5. In a bowl, whisk egg yolks, sugar, and cornstarch. Gradually add hot milk while whisking.
  6. Return to saucepan and cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until thickened.
  7. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla, and let cool completely.
  8. Assemble: Spread cooled custard in baked crust.
  9. Arrange fresh fruits on top in an attractive pattern.
  10. Brush with warmed apricot jam for a glossy finish.
  11. Chill for at least 2 hours before serving.
