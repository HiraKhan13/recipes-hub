Avocado Toast

Prep Time: 5 minutes Cook Time: 3 minutes Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole grain bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons microgreens or sprouts (optional)
  • 2 eggs (optional, for protein)

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread until golden brown and crispy.
  2. While bread is toasting, cut the avocado in half and remove the pit.
  3. Scoop the avocado flesh into a small bowl and mash with a fork.
  4. Add lemon juice, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes to the mashed avocado.
  5. Mix well until smooth but still slightly chunky.
  6. Spread the avocado mixture evenly on each slice of toast.
  7. Drizzle with olive oil and garnish with microgreens if desired.
  8. For extra protein, top with a poached or fried egg.
  9. Serve immediately while the toast is still warm and crispy.
